A family doctor for every stage of life

Many of our families have three and four generations in our care. We see general practice as one continuing consultation, from pregnancy and newborns, through school and adolescence, to parenthood, healthy ageing and later life.

  1. Pregnancy

    Antenatal care and Mater shared care, with support from your first appointment.

  2. Babies and toddlers

    Immunisations and bulk billed developmental checks as your child grows.

  3. School years

    Coughs, cuts and sport injuries, with children 12 and under bulk billed on weekdays.

  4. Teens and young adults

    A confidential place to talk about health, mood and the changes of growing up.

  5. Midlife

    Health assessments, skin checks and catching changes in your 40s and 50s early.

  6. Later life

    Care plans, medication reviews and support to stay independent for longer.

Your first visit, step by step

New to Sunstate? Here is what to expect, from booking to your follow-up.

Book your first appointment
  1. 1

    Book online or call

    Book with HotDoc at any time, or call our reception team on (07) 3207 7744.

  2. 2

    Fill in your new patient forms

    HotDoc sends you any forms you need before your visit. If you prefer, you can complete them in person when you arrive.

  3. 3

    Meet our nurse

    Most new patients see a nurse first. They check your forms, record your baseline observations and allergies, and tell you about all our services.

  4. 4

    See your doctor

    Your GP has time to listen, examine and explain. You decide together on any tests, treatment or follow-up appointments.

  5. 5

    Book ahead and claim on the spot

    Reception helps you book your next appointment and processes your Medicare claim through Tyro, so your rebate is paid straight away.

Caring professionally, since 2009

Our mission is to provide the highest standard of patient care whilst incorporating a holistic approach toward diagnosis and management of illness. We are committed to promoting health, wellbeing and disease prevention to all patients. We do not discriminate in the provision of excellent care, and we aim to treat all patients with dignity and respect.

Our vision is to provide comprehensive and continuing, safe, high quality patient centered health care, in a respectful, ethical and caring environment to Victoria Point and surrounding areas. We have longer standard appointments to enable us to provide the best possible care to our patients.

Our values centre around building and maintaining a patient focused culture and reputation as an “Employer of Choice” nurturing a team of highly motivated health care professionals focused on delivering exceptional patient service in all areas, working in a collaborative and supportive environment which is continually evolving. We are focused on improving patient outcomes by applying Collaborative methodology (PDSA model) to target gaps in care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you bulk bill?

Sunstate Family Practice is a private billing practice. Children 12 years and under are bulk billed for standard GP consultations between 8am and 5pm Monday to Friday. DVA Gold and White card holders are bulk billed for eligible conditions, and care plans, health assessments, home medication reviews, heart health assessments and immunisations are bulk billed. See our fees.

Do you have female doctors?

Yes. Many of our GPs are women, including doctors with interests in women’s health, antenatal care and children’s health. You can choose your doctor when you book online.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes, new patients are welcome. Book online with HotDoc or call us on (07) 3207 7744. HotDoc will send you any forms to complete before your first visit.

How long is a standard appointment?

We allow 15 minutes for standard appointments. If you have several things to discuss, book a longer appointment so your doctor can give each one the time it needs.

Do I need a referral for a skin check?

No. You don’t need a referral to see a GP for a skin check. Book online and choose a skin check or Total Body Mapping appointment, or call reception.

Can I get my Medicare rebate on the day?

Yes. Payment is made at the time of your appointment and reception can process your Medicare claim through our Tyro facility, so your rebate is paid back straight away.

Who do I call after hours?

For after-hours care, call 134 100. In an emergency, call 000.
