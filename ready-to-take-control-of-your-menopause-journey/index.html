Ready To Take Control Of Your Menopause Journey?

Menopause is a transition most women aren’t fully prepared for.

One day you’re cruising through life, and the next you’re waking up drenched in sweat, losing your train of thought, and staring at the ceiling at 3am wondering why everything suddenly feels different.

If you’re experiencing symptoms that are disrupting your daily life, whether it’s temperature changes, memory issues, or sleep disturbances, you are definitely not alone.

At Sunstate Family Practice, we support women through this transition every single day, so we understand just how overwhelming these changes can feel.

Here’s the reassuring part: menopause becomes so much more manageable with the right guidance, and that’s exactly what we’re here to provide.

Menopause symptoms are like snowflakes, no two women experience them exactly the same way.

The classic hits:

  • Hot flashes and night sweats (because who doesn’t love waking up feeling like they’ve run a marathon?)
  • Brain fog and memory lapses
  • Sleep changes and disturbances (3am wake-up calls, anyone?)
  • Mood swings that would make a teenager jealous
  • Irregular or heavy periods
  • Joint aches and stiffness

The surprise guests:

  • Changes in libido
  • Weight gain, especially around the middle
  • Increased anxiety or depression
  • Headaches
  • Digestive issues

These symptoms don’t just affect your physical health, they impact work, relationships, and overall quality of life.

That’s exactly why professional support matters.

Think of it as your personal roadmap through this transition. It’s not just a quick chat, it’s comprehensive care designed specifically for women experiencing menopausal changes.

If you’re an existing Sunstate patient, you can book a dedicated Menopause Assessment with your preferred GP. For more information about what to expect, you can also check out our menopause health assessment flyer.

Here’s what is included in your assessment:

  1. Determine pre-, peri- or post-menopausal status:Are you just starting this journey, in the thick of it, or coming out the other side? Knowing where you are helps guide treatment decisions and expectations.
  2. Symptom check score: We use validated assessment tools to evaluate how symptoms affect your daily life. This gives us a clear picture so we can help effectively.
  3. Treatment options: non-pharmacological and pharmacological: Gone are the days when your only options were “grin and bear it” or basic hormone therapy.
  4. Consider important health screenings:Your assessment will review whether you need:
  • Cervical screening
  • Mammography
  • Bone densitometry (because nobody wants osteoporosis later)

Non-pharmacological options:

  • Dietary adjustments for symptom management
  • Exercise routines that work for your lifestyle
  • Stress management techniques
  • Sleep hygiene strategies that actually work

Pharmacological treatments:

  • Evidence-based medications
  • Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) when appropriate

We’ll discuss the risks and benefits of all options, so you can make informed decisions about your care.

5. Mental health check-in:Hormonal changes can trigger anxiety, depression, or mood swings that feel completely out of character. This isn’t weakness, it’s biology.

We check your emotional wellbeing and connect you with support when needed.

6. Your personal management plan:You leave with a clear, practical plan tailored specifically to you. No cookie-cutter approaches, just evidence-based strategies that fit your life.

There’s a lot of menopause information out there, and not all of it’s reliable.

Your friend’s miracle cure might not work for you. That influencer’s supplement stack might not be evidence-based (or safe).

For evidence-based information about menopause, the Australian Government’s Health Direct provides comprehensive resources about symptoms, treatments, and what to expect.

Professional guidance helps you:

  • Navigate the noise and focus on treatments that actually work
  • Make informed decisions based on current research
  • Monitor your health during this transition
  • Adjust your plan as needs change
  • Address complications before they become bigger problems

At Sunstate Family Practice, menopause isn’t just a medical condition to manage, it’s a life transition that deserves respect, understanding, and comprehensive care.

Our experienced GPs stay current with latest menopause research and offer evidence-based options including lifestyle modifications, non-hormonal treatments, and HRT where appropriate.

As part of our comprehensive women’s health services, we understand the unique health needs women face throughout their lives.

We take time to listen (15-minute standard appointments), and we’re happy to book longer consultations when needed.

Every woman’s experience is different. What works for your sister might not work for you, and that’s okay.

We’re here to find what works for YOUR body and YOUR life.
