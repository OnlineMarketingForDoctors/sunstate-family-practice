Featured services

Learn more about the services our patients ask about most.

  • Skin Cancer Checks

    Yearly skin checks are essential as Queensland has one of the highest skin cancer rates in the world.

  • Total Body Mapping

    Mole mapping and advanced total body photography that tracks your skin over time.

  • Ear Micro Suction

    Microscopic ear suction is the safest way to remove a buildup of wax or other objects from your ear canal.

  • Iron Infusions

    We have doctors who are experienced in performing Iron Infusions at our practice.

  • Preventive Health

    A proactive, collaborative, patient centered approach to health care.

  • Travel Medicine

    Protect yourself and your family from disease and infection before you travel.

  • Chronic Disease Management

    Care plans and team care coordinated by our nurses with your GP and allied health.

  • Skin Checks Redland Bay

    Comprehensive skin checks for patients from Redland Bay, minutes from the practice.
