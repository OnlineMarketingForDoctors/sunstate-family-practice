Your Rights

Whilst we endeavour to provide the highest quality health care and services, if you have any concerns, we would like to hear about it. Please ask to speak to the Practice Manager or discuss with your doctor. You could request a feedback form and use the Suggestion Box, or contact the Practice Manager directly via phone: 3207 7744 or email: manager@sunstatefamilypractice.com.au

If you would like to seek further advice or feel that your concerns have not been resolved by the practice, you can contact:

Office of the Health Ombudsman
Telephone: 133 646
Web: www.oho.qld.gov.au

Members of the public may make a notification to the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA) www.ahpra.gov.au, about the conduct, health or performance of a practitioner.
