Chronic Disease Management

Chronic disease is one of Australia’s leading causes of illness, disability, and death. At Sunstate Family Practice & Skin Cancer Clinic, our patient-centred, proactive, collaborative, approach to chronic disease management health care gives our patients the chance to reduce their exposure and risk to chronic conditions.

The Australian Institute of Health and Welfare identifies 8 major chronic diseases affecting Australians. They are:

  1. Arthritis
  2. Asthma
  3. Back Pain
  4. Cancer
  5. Cardiovascular diseases
  6. Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease
  7. Diabetes
  8. Mental Health conditions

Your GP and clinical care team are here to help you and your family stay healthy so you can lead long healthy active lives. To book an appointment for a general health assessment or to discuss any health concerns please contact the clinic.

Phone us on (07) 3207 7744 or Book Online.

How we help you manage a chronic condition

  1. 1

    A GP management plan

    Your GP works with you to set goals for your condition and the care you need to reach them.

  2. 2

    Team care arrangements

    Our nurses coordinate your care with allied health professionals and specialists, including Medicare rebateable referrals where you are eligible.

  3. 3

    Regular reviews

    We review your plan regularly so your care keeps up with your health.

    Care plans and health assessments are bulk billed Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. See our fees.
