At Sunstate Family Practice, our comprehensive Total Body Mapping (TBM) service combines advanced total body photography with expert skin examination and ongoing comparison of your skin over time.

What is the difference?

You may hear this service referred to as:

✔ Mole Mapping

✔ Advanced Total Body Photography

✔ Total Body Mapping (TBM)

These terms are often used interchangeably. At Sunstate Family Practice, we use the name Total Body Mapping (TBM) to describe our advanced total body photography service combined with expert skin examination and long-term monitoring.

What is Total Body Mapping (Mole Mapping)

Why People Choose Mole Mapping & Advanced Total Body Photography

  • You’ve got lots of moles or freckles, and it’s hard to remember what’s changed and what hasn’t.
  • You’ve had heavy sun exposure over the years, whether through work, sport, hobbies, or just a love of the sun.
  • You’ve got a personal or family history of melanoma and prefer closer monitoring of your skin.
  • Your GP suggested it to support follow-up planning and ongoing monitoring.
  • Some areas are hard to see, such as the back, shoulders, and scalp. TBM means like-for-like photos can be reviewed between visits.

What You Get With Mole Mapping & Total Body Photography

When you book TBM alongside your skin check, you leave with practical tools that make the next visit easier:

A Clear Baseline

You’ve got lots of moles or freckles, and it’s hard to remember what’s changed and what hasn’t.

Context in Your Notes

Your GP adds monitoring notes linked to images – what’s new, what’s unchanged, and what to watch.

A Plan You Understand

Before you go, your GP explains what to watch for, when to come back, and what would prompt an earlier review.

Secure Storage

Images are date-stamped and stored with your medical record, not on personal devices.

Why Mole Mapping & Total Body Photography Help Detect Change Early (and Why Phone Photos Don't)

TBM Images

  • Same angles, distance, and framing every time
  • Consistent lighting and neutral background
  • Reliable comparison - differences are from your skin, not the camera
  • Steady reference point stored securely

Phone Photos

  • Varying lighting (bathroom, sun, flash)
  • Different angles and zoom levels
  • Spots can look larger, smaller, darker, or lighter
  • Scattered throughout camera roll

What's Involved in a Mole Mapping Appointment

  1. 1

    Image Capture

    A trained team member takes TBM photos calmly and methodically.

  2. 2

    Skin Check

    Your GP examines your skin using a dermatoscope and discusses any spots to watch.

  3. 3

    Your Plan

    Your GP explains their findings, answers questions, and discusses future follow-ups.

Before You Come In

  • No Makeup, SPF or Sunscreen Products or Fake Tans on your skin.
  • Wear Plain Coloured Underwear
  • No Nail Polish or Jewellery
  • For Men: Brief Styled underpants are preferred over trunks
  • For Ladies: we prefer you to remove your bra (Optional)
  • Bring notes about any spots that concern you.
  • Tell us about comfort or modesty preferences. We respect your privacy and aim to make your experience as comfortable as possible.
