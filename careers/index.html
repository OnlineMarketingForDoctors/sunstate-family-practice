About the business and the role About Us

Sunstate was established in 2009 and, since then has grown to a thriving General Practice with 9 GPs. We are a registrar training practice committed to providing a comprehensive, proactive, high-quality health service using a systematic approach to chronic disease management and preventative health care.

Job tasks and responsibilities

Treatment room + CDM Nurse

We require a dynamic nurse to join our dedicated nursing team to perform all aspects of General Practice including but not limited to:Sterilisation and infection controlCold Chain managementHotDoc Recall and RemindersCare PlansHealth AssessmentsChronic Disease ManagementPatient Education & Health PromotionEar syringingECG & ABI DopplerNIP and Travel vaccinationsVenesectionDressingsRegistrar trainingPromotion of Sunstate ServicesCompliance 5th Edition RACGP StandardsCompliance Sunstate Policy and Procedures Skills and experience

The successful candidate will possess well-developed communication skills and a caring, calm and highly organised approach. Familiarity and understanding of CDM and preventative health MBS items, and experience performing high-quality care plans and health assessments is highly regarded but not essential. This position would also suit a new graduate who is interested in specialising in general practice. Reporting to the Nurse Team Leader, this role entails working both autonomously and as part of a collaborative team of doctors, nurses and administration staff.

AdvantageousExperience using Cubiko, Hotdoc, Best Practice, Microsoft Office SuiteInvolvement in CDM programs, Continuing Quality Improvement activities, CDM Clinics Job benefits and perks

SUNSTATE CULTURE

We pride ourselves on upholding our reputation as an Employer of Choice. In your role, you will be supported by the practice owners, practice manager, doctors, allied health, reception staff and Nurse Team Leader. CPD activities are arranged in-house for your convenience. Regular clinical and team meetings are scheduled to optimise workflow and ensure a collaborative approach is applied to all improvement activities in order to achieve the best possible outcomes for our patients. Opportunities will be regularly provided to discuss any workplace matters including job satisfaction. Management's 'open door' policy encourages a healthy and positive team culture by giving everyone a voice.

VR GP Position – Full time/Part time

Read the role description

Sunstate Family Practice & Skin Cancer Clinic is a well-established, busy, innovative, private billing GP owned practice, located at Victoria Point, opposite the YMCA.

We are committed to nurturing a friendly, relaxed & professional team culture.

Our GP practice owner, Dr Andrew Ganter, is an experienced GP Registrar Supervisor so this position would suit an early career GP seeking a supportive continuous learning environment OR an experienced GP interested in being involved in Registrar Supervision.

Sunstate GP’s are very well supported by a collaborative team including their peers, an experienced Practice Manager and their highly effective reception and nursing teams.

Our team delivers a non-corporate, high quality care model of service provision, with a traditional family practice approach, incorporating all the innovative technology currently available on the market to maximise performance and income for doctors and staff.

Established systems for chronic disease management utilise a spacious well-equipped treatment room, and Care Plan Nurse room, to help make your day run smoothly.

A full private paying patient base is immediately available for at least one or two doctors, working full or part time.
