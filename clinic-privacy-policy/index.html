Clinic Privacy Policy

Sunstate Family Practice is committed to ensuring your personal information is professionally managed in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This privacy policy explains how your personal information (including health information) is collected and used within our practice, and the circumstances in which it is shared with third parties.

When you register as a patient of our practice, your doctor and/or their support team will need to collect personal information to provide you with quality healthcare services. We also use it for directly related business activities, such as financial claims and payments, practice audits/accreditation, and routine business processes. Only practice staff who need to see your personal information can access it. All staff have signed a Confidentiality Agreement as part of their employment contract.

Personal information collected includes:

  • Name, address, date of birth, contact details (including next of kin and emergency contact)
  • Medical information: medical history, medications, allergies, adverse events, immunisations, social history, family history, risk factors, and details of treatments or operations
  • Medicare or DVA numbers for identification and claiming purposes
  • Healthcare Identifiers
  • Health Fund Details
  • Pension, Health Care, and Senior Card numbers

Our practice may collect your information in a range of ways:

  1. When you first make an appointment, our staff will ask you to complete our New Patient form.
  2. During the course of providing medical services, including receiving records from previous practices and correspondence from other healthcare providers and My Health Record.
  3. When you interact with us (website visits, email, phone calls, appointments, SMS reminders). Medical information is only communicated by email if you have given consent, as it is not secure.
  4. If we cannot collect information directly from you, we may collect it from your guardian/responsible person, other healthcare providers, hospitals, community health services, diagnostic services, or similar sources.

You have the right to deal with us anonymously or under a pseudonym unless that would be impractical, or unless required by law to deal with identified individuals. Patients who do not provide identity documents must pay for consultations in full and are ineligible for a Medicare rebate.

We may share your personal information in the following circumstances:

  • With other healthcare providers, including third-party service providers such as IT and transcription vendors (including secure Artificial Intelligence (AI) transcription and documentation support systems).
  • With third parties who work with our practice for business-related purposes such as IT providers and accreditation agencies.
  • Where required by statutory law, such as mandatory notification of certain diseases.
  • Where necessary to prevent serious threats to a patient’s life, health, or safety, or to public health or safety, where it is impractical to obtain consent.
  • In response to court subpoenas or as otherwise authorised by law.
  • During the course of providing medical services (e.g., Electronic Transfer of Prescriptions, My Health Record System).
  • To assist in locating a missing person.
  • To establish, exercise or defend an equitable claim.
  • For confidential dispute resolution processes.
  • With third parties to whom you have given written consent, such as insurance companies, Workcover, and lawyers.

We will not use your personal information for direct marketing without your express consent. If you do consent, you may opt out at any time by notifying our practice in writing. We do not share your information outside Australia without your consent, unless required by law or under exceptional, legally permitted circumstances.

Personal information is securely stored at our practice, primarily in electronic format. Any paper correspondence is scanned into your record, then shredded. Imaging such as X-rays, CT, and MRI scans are the patient’s responsibility and are generally not retained. Documented photos are stored electronically.

Security measures include unique passwords, confidentiality agreements for all staff and contractors, and encrypted daily backups managed on-site and by IT consultants.

Our practice may use a secure, healthcare-specific Artificial Intelligence (AI) platform during consultations solely as a listening tool for transcription purposes. The aim is to assist with medical documentation so your practitioner can focus on you during your consultation.

Key points about AI use:

  • The AI system is not used to deliver healthcare or make clinical decisions.
  • The AI platform complies with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
  • Identifiable health information is not stored by the AI provider beyond what is necessary to generate the transcript.
  • All transcripts and summaries generated by AI are reviewed and edited by your treating practitioner before they become part of your official medical record.

We take reasonable steps to correct personal information that is inaccurate or outdated. We may ask you to verify your information periodically.

You have the right to access and correct your information. Requests should be made in writing to:

Practice Manager, Sunstate Family Practice, 127 Link Road, Victoria Point QLD 4165
Email: manager@sunstatefamilypractice.com.au

We will process your request within 30 days. Administrative or photocopying fees may apply.

If you have concerns about privacy or wish to lodge a complaint about a breach, contact us in writing with full details and supporting information. We will investigate and notify you of the outcome within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied, contact the Office of the Health Ombudsman:

Telephone: 133 646
Postal Address: GPO Box 13281, George Street, Brisbane QLD 4001
Email: complaints@oho.qld.gov.au

This Privacy Policy is regularly reviewed to ensure ongoing compliance with legislation and best practice guidance. Updates will be notified via our website or in-practice signage.
